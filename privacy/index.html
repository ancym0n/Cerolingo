Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Overview of Data Practices

Cerolingo is designed with user privacy in mind. The extension operates strictly on the client side inside your browser. It does not collect, log, intercept, or transmit personal information, login credentials, or browsing activity to any external server.

2. Local Storage & Scope of Execution

The extension uses local browser storage (chrome.storage.local and localStorage) only to remember your preferences, such as enabled features, menu position, and the last completed story URL. This data stays on your device and is never uploaded to external servers. All automation and page modifications happen locally within your browser tab.

3. Third-Party Interactions

Cerolingo only runs on official Duolingo domains (duolingo.com and duolingo.cn). It does not include any third-party analytics, tracking scripts, advertising libraries, or external network connections.

4. Core Compliance Declarations

In accordance with browser store policies and privacy regulations, Cerolingo guarantees the following:

  • No Data Collection or Sale: Personal information and user settings are never collected, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Single Purpose Scope: Requested browser permissions are used solely to provide the in-page menu and lesson automation on Duolingo.
  • No Tracking: The extension runs without analytics or background tracking, keeping your browsing activity private.

5. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if new features or technical changes are introduced. Any updates will be published on this page.
