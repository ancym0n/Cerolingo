FAQ

1. What is your Privacy Policy?

Cerolingo respects your privacy. The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, account credentials, or browsing history. All settings are saved locally on your device using browser storage (chrome.storage.local) and are never sent to external servers or shared with third parties.

View Privacy Policy

2. What is Cerolingo?

Cerolingo is a browser extension built to automate lessons and stories on Duolingo. Unlike older standalone tools, it runs directly inside your web browser and integrates into the Duolingo interface.

3. What are its main features?

Cerolingo includes an Auto Solver for both standard lessons and stories, optional Blue Animations for automated actions, Audio Blocking to skip spoken dialogue delays, a Lessons Highlighter on the learning path, a Legendary Bypass, and built-in Lesson Loop and XP Farming modes.

4. How do I use the Mod Menu?

The Mod Menu appears directly on the Duolingo page so you can switch modes and adjust settings without opening the extension popup. You can click the top title bar at any time to minimize the menu, or drag it anywhere on your screen on both desktop and mobile.

5. Which platforms are supported?

Cerolingo is available for Google Chrome, other Chromium-based browsers, Mozilla Firefox on desktop, and Firefox for Android. It supports both duolingo.com and duolingo.cn.
