FAQ

1. What is your Privacy Policy?

Cerolingo respects user privacy. The extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, account credentials, or browsing history. All configuration settings are kept strictly on your local device using local browser storage (chrome.storage.local). No data is ever synced to cloud servers, tracked, or shared with third parties. View Privacy Policy

2. What is Cerolingo?

Cerolingo is a lightweight browser extension designed as an automation and customization utility for Duolingo, running directly inside your web browser to streamline your learning experience.

3. What are its main features?

Cerolingo includes an Auto-Answer Bot for story lessons, an instant Audio Blocking mode to skip talking animations, Story Map Highlighting, a Legendary Lesson Bypass, a Point Exploit, and a full All-in-One Mod Menu to control everything directly on the page.

4. How do I use the Mod Menu?

The Mod Menu overlays directly on Duolingo so you can toggle modules and change settings without opening your browser's extension popup. You can double-click the title bar anytime to minimize or hide the menu, and drag it around smoothly on both desktop and mobile screens.

5. Which platforms are supported?

Cerolingo is available as a browser extension for Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox (desktop), and Firefox for Android mobile. It fully supports both duolingo.com and the Chinese version, duolingo.cn.
